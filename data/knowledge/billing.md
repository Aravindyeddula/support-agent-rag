# Billing and refunds

## Subscription changes
Nimbus is a fictional workflow automation service used in this demonstration. A workspace owner can change a subscription from Workspace Settings, then Billing. Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of the next billing cycle. Existing data is retained when a workspace changes plans.

## Refund eligibility
A first subscription payment can be reviewed for a refund within 14 days of purchase if the workspace has used fewer than 100 workflow runs. Renewal charges are reviewed individually by the billing team. Support must collect the invoice ID and workspace ID, then prepare a billing escalation. A support agent cannot approve or issue a refund.

## Duplicate charges
If two completed payments appear for the same invoice, collect both payment references and the invoice ID. Escalate the case to billing with high priority. Pending card authorizations are not completed payments and normally disappear within seven business days. Do not ask customers to send full card numbers.
